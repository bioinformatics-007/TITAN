TITAN: A dual-arm learning framework for robust biological sequence prediction and ranking

TITAN is a machine-learning framework designed for biological sequence analysis and prediction. The framework integrates sequence-derived representations, physicochemical features, graph-based information, ensemble learning, calibration, and uncertainty-aware prediction to provide robust and interpretable predictions across biological datasets.

Overview

TITAN combines complementary biological information through a dual-arm architecture:

Sequence-space arm — captures information from sequence-derived representations and embeddings.
Physicochemical-space arm — incorporates physicochemical and tabular molecular features.
Graph-based representations — capture relationships and structural/topological information between samples.
Ensemble prediction — integrates classification, regression, and ranking signals.
Calibration and uncertainty estimation — improves the reliability and interpretability of model predictions.
Ranking and generalization evaluation — evaluates performance on held-out and external datasets.
